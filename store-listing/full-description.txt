Two classic puzzle games in one app, with no ads and no internet required.

WordQuest pairs a relaxed word search with a quick-fire trivia quiz across eight themed collections — Animals, Geography, Movies & TV, Science, Sports, Food & Cooking, History, and Music. Every theme holds 1,000 levels in each mode, so there is always another puzzle waiting.

WORD SEARCH
Drag across the grid to trace hidden words. Puzzles start gentle at 8x8 with five words and grow to 14x14 with ten as you climb, so the challenge builds at your pace. Stuck on the last word? Spend a hint to reveal it — though hints get scarcer in the harder tiers.

TRIVIA
Five questions per level against the clock. Build a streak for a higher score, and keep a 50:50 lifeline in reserve for the question that catches you out. When you leave the app mid-round, a live notification keeps your score and countdown on screen.

LOCAL MULTIPLAYER
Play head-to-head trivia with up to eight people in the same room. Devices find each other over Bluetooth or your local Wi-Fi automatically — no accounts, no room codes, and no internet connection. One player hosts, everyone answers the same questions, and the final standings settle it.

PROGRESS
Levels unlock as you play, grouped into ten difficulty tiers of 100. Earn up to three stars on a level by finishing under par time without spending a hint, collect coins along the way, and pick up where you left off in any theme.

MADE TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY
• No ads of any kind
• No analytics or tracking
• No account or sign-up
• No internet needed to play
• Works fully offline, start to finish

The app follows your system light or dark setting, or you can pin it to either. Sound effects and haptic feedback each have their own switch in Settings.

Every puzzle is generated from curated word and question pools, so the same level always plays the same way — compare a score with a friend and you are comparing the same puzzle.
